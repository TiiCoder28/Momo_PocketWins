## 10. Detailed Project Description

PocketWins is a personalised behavioural finance Mini App designed for the MoMo ecosystem. It helps users understand not only where their money goes, but how their own financial behaviour changes over time.

Unlike a standard budgeting chatbot that gives the same generic advice repeatedly, PocketWins builds a structured picture of each user's spending history, goals, self-reported choices, and progress. It can identify trends such as reduced overspending, improved budget adherence, recurring impulse-spending patterns, and categories where the user's behaviour is improving.

PocketWins measures users primarily against their own historical behaviour rather than comparing them with other people. The goal is to reward progress instead of demanding financial perfection.

The core experience follows a simple loop: Plan → Spend → Reflect → Recognise → Pay Yourself → Save → Review → Repeat.

Users can create a basic money plan and see what is safe to spend after essential commitments. They can reflect on purchases as planned, flexible, impulse, regretted, or avoided. Positive actions can then become optional micro-saving moments through the "Pay Yourself" feature.

For example, a user who stays within their plan, cooks instead of ordering takeaway, avoids an impulse purchase, completes a personal goal, or reaches a milestone can choose to pay a small amount such as R5, R10, or R20 toward a personal goal. Over time, PocketWins shows how those small behavioural wins translate into financial progress.

Historical analytics calculate reliable trends such as month-over-month overspend, streaks, category changes, avoided purchases, and amounts paid toward goals. An AI layer can then explain those verified insights conversationally and provide personalised guidance without inventing the underlying calculations.

PocketWins is designed to live inside MoMo so that financial planning, behavioural reflection, payments, and goal progress can exist close to the user's everyday money experience without requiring a separate standalone application.
