# Product Vision

## Product

**PocketWins**

**Tagline:** *Turn small everyday wins into savings.*

## Problem

Most budgeting tools explain what happened after money has already been spent. Their advice can also become generic and repetitive: spend less, save more, avoid impulse purchases.

Real financial behaviour is more personal. Different people overspend for different reasons, improve at different rates, care about different goals, and respond to different forms of encouragement.

PocketWins is designed around that reality.

## Vision

PocketWins aims to become a personalized behavioural finance layer inside MoMo: a companion that understands how an individual user's money habits change over time and helps them convert positive behavioural progress into tangible financial progress.

The product should not judge users or demand financial perfection. It should help users compare themselves primarily against their own past behaviour, recognise improvement, discover patterns, and build momentum.

## Core value proposition

PocketWins helps users:

1. Understand what they can safely spend after essential commitments.
2. Reflect on spending decisions without shame or financial scolding.
3. Recognise personal spending patterns and behavioural triggers over time.
4. Measure improvement against their own previous behaviour.
5. Reward positive choices by "paying themselves" small amounts toward personal goals.
6. See weekly and monthly progress in a way that feels meaningful and motivating.

## Example of personalization

A generic budgeting assistant might say:

> You overspent this month. Try to reduce your expenses next month.

PocketWins should instead be capable of saying:

> You went R210 over your flexible-spending budget this month, down from R470 last month and R620 the month before. Your discretionary overspend has improved for three consecutive months.

The insight should be calculated from structured historical data. AI may explain the insight conversationally, but should not invent the underlying trend.

## Target users

PocketWins is aimed at everyday MoMo users who want greater control over their money without needing a complex finance application or formal financial-planning knowledge.

The concept is intentionally adaptable across income levels. Progress may mean saving R20 for one person and R2,000 for another. The product measures momentum rather than enforcing a universal definition of financial success.

## Why MoMo?

PocketWins belongs inside MoMo because the behaviour it is trying to improve happens around money itself.

Instead of asking users to install a separate budgeting application, manually copy transactions, and then move elsewhere to act on recommendations, PocketWins can sit close to the payment experience: helping users plan, reflect, make better decisions, and potentially move money toward their goals within the same ecosystem.

## Core product loop

**Plan → Spend → Reflect → Recognise → Pay Yourself → Save → Review → Repeat**

## "Pay Yourself" concept

Positive behaviour can become a micro-saving trigger.

Examples:

- Stayed within today's plan → pay yourself R5
- Avoided an impulse purchase → pay yourself R20
- Cooked instead of ordering takeaway → pay yourself R15
- Reached a personal goal such as 10,000 steps → pay yourself R10
- Completed a difficult personal task → pay yourself a custom amount

Users decide which behaviours matter to them and how much they want to reward those behaviours.

The product should never imply that users are required to save money they cannot afford to save.

## Longer-term vision

Over time, PocketWins could build a personalised behavioural profile that understands:

- spending trends
- recurring categories
- periods of higher discretionary spending
- user-labelled impulse or regretted purchases
- successful budgeting strategies
- savings goals
- personal reward rules
- streaks and consistency
- behavioural progress across weeks and months

This allows PocketWins to become increasingly relevant to the individual instead of repeating the same generic advice every day.
